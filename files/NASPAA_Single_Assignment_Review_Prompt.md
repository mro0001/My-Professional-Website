knowledge of NASPAA (Network of Schools of Public Policy, Affairs and Administration)

  ● STRONG — The assignment directly and substantively engages this domain.

- Apply public management organization theories

- Manage information and networks

- Apply risk management principles
- Resolve conflict through negotiation and consensus-building

DOMAIN 3: TO ANALYZE, SYNTHESIZE, THINK CRITICALLY, SOLVE PROBLEMS, AND MAKE

  program, and individual performance

  recommendations

- Identify and analyze ethical dilemmas involving fiduciary stewardship of
  public resources
- Know the meanings of due process, authority, and social equity and their
  role in democratic governance

- Distinguish short- from long-term fiscal consequences of program and policy
  decisions
- Exercise ethical responsibility when conducting research and making decisions
- Identify short- and long-term impacts of decisions on the physical environment

DOMAIN 5: TO COMMUNICATE AND INTERACT PRODUCTIVELY AND IN CULTURALLY RESPONSIVE
WAYS WITH A DIVERSE AND CHANGING WORKFORCE AND SOCIETY AT LARGE

- Demonstrate interpersonal communication skills to serve diverse populations

- Demonstrate flexibility and adaptability
- Demonstrate facilitation skills
- Build actionable consensus

- Recognize and adapt to cultural differences
